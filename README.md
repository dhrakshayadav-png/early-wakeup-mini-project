# **Early Wake-Up, Exercise & Health Outcomes**

## **1) Objective**
* To study the effect of early wake-up and exercise on health
* To analyze physical and mental well-being improvements
* To understand lifestyle impact on productivity

## **2) Dataset**
* Wake-up time
* Sleep hours
* Exercise duration
* Type of exercise
* Energy level 
* Stress level
* Productivity score
  
## **3) Tools Used**
* Python (Pandas, NumPy)
* Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook
* Excel / Google Sheets
* Matplotlib & Seaborn

## **4) Project Description**
* Analyze daily habits and health outcomes
* Compare early risers vs late risers
* Find relationship between exercise and health
* Use graphs to show patterns

## **5) Steps**
* Collect data
* Load dataset in Python
* Clean data (remove errors/missing values)
* Perform EDA (analysis)
* Visualize results using graphs
* Draw insights

## **6) Benefits**
* Improves health awareness
* Reduces stress
* Increases productivity
* Helps in weight control
* Builds discipline and routine

## **7) Conclusion**
* Early wake-up + exercise improves health
* Boosts mental and physical fitness
* Increases energy and focus
* Helps maintain a healthy lifestyle.
