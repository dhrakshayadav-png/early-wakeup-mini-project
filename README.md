**Early Wake-Up, Exercise & Health Outcomes**
**1) Objective**

The main objective of this project is to analyze how early wake-up habits combined with regular exercise impact human health, productivity, and mental well-being.
It aims to understand whether people who wake up early and exercise regularly have better health outcomes compared to those who do not.

**2) Dataset**
For this project, a sample dataset can include:

 Sleep time (hours slept per day)
Wake-up time
Exercise duration (minutes per day)
Type of exercise (walking, running, gym, yoga)
Daily energy level (1–10 scale)
Stress level (1–10 scale)
BMI (Body Mass Index)
Productivity score (self-reported)

👉 Dataset can be created using Excel, Google Sheets, or collected using a simple survey form (Google Forms).

**3) Tools Used**
Python (Pandas, NumPy)
Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook
Excel / Google Sheets 
Matplotlib & Seaborn 
Google Forms 
**4) Project Description**

This project studies the relationship between lifestyle habits (wake-up time and exercise) and health outcomes.
Data is collected or simulated and then analyzed using Python. 
Visualizations are used to identify patterns such as how early wake-up correlates with better energy levels, lower stress, and improved productivity.

The project helps in understanding how small daily habits can significantly affect long-term health.

**5) Steps**
Data Collection – Gather data using forms or Excel
Data Loading – Import dataset using Pandas
Data Cleaning – Handle missing or incorrect values
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) – Find patterns and relationships
Visualization – Use graphs (bar chart, scatter plot, histogram)
Insights Extraction – Interpret results
Conclusion Drawing – Summarize findings
**6) Benefits**
Improves awareness about healthy lifestyle habits
Encourages early wake-up discipline
Shows impact of exercise on mental health
Helps in better time management
Reduces stress and increases productivity
Supports fitness and weight control goals
**7) Conclusion**

The analysis clearly shows that individuals who wake up early and exercise regularly tend to have better physical health, improved mental stability, and higher productivity levels. These habits help in maintaining a balanced lifestyle and reducing stress. Therefore, adopting early wake-up routines combined with daily exercise can significantly improve overall health outcomes.**
